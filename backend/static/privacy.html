Enterprise Privacy Policy

NEXUS AI is built upon the foundational principle of complete customer data sovereignty. This document outlines how data is ingested, processed, protected, and deleted across our autonomous multi-agent state graphs.

Zero Model Training
Customer prompt inputs and RAG documents are never used to train or fine-tune public foundation models.
Full AES-256 Encryption
Episodic memory stores and pgvector embeddings are encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
Right to Purge
Instant one-click cryptographic erasure removes workspace embeddings and task telemetry permanently.

01. Data Categories & Collection Scope

When interacting with the NEXUS AI orchestration platform, we collect only the minimum telemetry necessary to execute autonomous multi-agent state graphs:

  • Ingested Documents: CSV datasets, financial tables, and PDF reports provided for schema extraction and semantic chunking.
  • Execution Telemetry: Agent reasoning steps, tool invocation metrics, latency percentiles, and quality audit verification scores.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Signatures: Operator identifiers, decision timestamps, and approval rationale for governance logging.
  • Speech Audio: Sub-300ms ephemeral voice snippets for intent extraction (discarded immediately after text conversion).

02. Zero Model Training & LLM Boundaries

We maintain strict contractual and technical barriers prohibiting any customer mission data from entering model training corpora. When specialized agents invoke neural kernels (e.g. Gemini 1.5 Pro, Flash, or sandboxed math engines), requests are processed under enterprise zero-data-retention (ZDR) agreements.

03. Vector Embeddings & Tenant Isolation

All semantic RAG embeddings generated by the Research Agent are segregated using Row-Level Security (RLS) within PostgreSQL pgvector. Cross-tenant vector leakage is mathematically impossible through cryptographically separated search namespaces.

04. PII & PHI Redaction Guardrails

Before documents are vectorized or routed to external agent nodes, the Security Sentry automatically applies Named Entity Recognition (NER) tokenization to mask Social Security Numbers, credit cards, bank accounts, and protected healthcare health identifiers (HIPAA compliance).
