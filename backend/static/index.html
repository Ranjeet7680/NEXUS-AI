FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Inquiries

Everything you need to know about NEXUS AI multi-agent orchestration.

How does NEXUS AI eliminate mathematical hallucinations?
NEXUS isolates arithmetic away from generative LLMs. Financial calculations, percentages, and variance metrics are executed inside deterministic sandboxed Python kernels with SHA-256 cryptographic audit logs, guaranteeing 0.0% calculation error.
What is the Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Gateway?
The HITL Gateway is a zero-trust interception checkpoint. Any action that alters critical state (sending emails, modifying customer databases, executing financial wires) is halted until an authorized human officer signs off with a timestamped cryptographic signature.
Can NEXUS run entirely on-premises without cloud dependencies?
Yes. The Sovereign deployment package can be installed on private Kubernetes clusters or air-gapped Docker instances utilizing local open-weights LLMs via vLLM, Ollama, or local embedding engines, ensuring zero telemetry leaves your private network.
How does the multilingual voice assistant function?
The voice interface connects directly over WebSockets and Web Speech APIs with sub-300ms speech-to-intent latency. Spoken directives in English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, or German are parsed into structured DAG operations and dispatched across the Swarm.
