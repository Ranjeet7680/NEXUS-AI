Master Service Agreement

These Master Service Terms govern the licensing, autonomous agent dispatch, Human-in-the-Loop governance protocols, and service level agreements (SLAs) of the NEXUS AI platform.

01. Multi-Agent Software Licensing & Dispatch

Subject to compliance with these Terms, NEXUS AI grants the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable enterprise license to orchestrate autonomous software agents across LangGraph-directed state machines. Customers may instantiate parallel agent workflows within the concurrency boundaries specified in their subscription tier.

02. Deterministic Mathematical Execution SLA

Unlike stochastic single-turn LLMs, NEXUS AI executes arithmetic, financial variance reconciliations, and regulatory calculations inside isolated sandboxed Python execution kernels.

GUARANTEED ZERO-MATH-HALLUCINATION COMMITMENT:
All calculations verified by the Quality Auditor agent carry a 100% deterministic accuracy guarantee against gold-standard GAAP and IEEE 754 floating point arithmetic.

03. 100% Customer Deliverables Ownership

The Customer retains exclusive and unencumbered ownership of all intellectual property, data outputs, analytical reports, visual node graphs, and synthesized deliverables generated by the Swarm. NEXUS AI claims zero ownership or licensing rights over customer-generated artifacts.

04. Human-in-the-Loop Governance Responsibilities

For Tier-1 operational actions (such as automated external wires, customer-facing email broadcasts, or database schema alterations), the NEXUS platform enforces an interception gate. The Customer is responsible for designating authorized officers with valid cryptographic signing credentials to approve or reject pending items in the Approvals queue.

05. Platform Uptime & Disaster Recovery SLA

For Enterprise Business and Sovereign Cloud tiers, NEXUS AI commits to a 99.9% monthly service uptime. Scheduled maintenance windows will be communicated at least 48 hours in advance, and state graph execution snapshots are redundantly backed up every 60 seconds across geographically distributed availability zones.

06. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Customers agree not to utilize the multi-agent engine to:

  • Design autonomous cyber-weapons, denial-of-service attack scripts, or malware.
  • Bypass human-in-the-loop safeguards on life-critical healthcare clinical triage.
  • Conduct deceptive market manipulation or unauthorized mass surveillance.
